# ORCA Player v0.2.0: Phase 2, core player

## What's new

- **Local files.** Folder view and all-videos view, as a list or a grid (three sizes), with thumbnails made in the background and cached, duration and resolution badges, a thin resume progress bar, and a "new" mark on videos never played. Sort by name (natural order), date, size or length; filter videos, audio or both; search inside the tab. MediaStore is scanned and watched for changes. Folders that MediaStore doesn't index (such as some SD card folders) can be added with the file picker, and folders with a `.nomedia` file are skipped. Android 14 partial access is handled with an "Allow more" prompt.
- **Player.** One ExoPlayer in a MediaSessionService, so rotation, folding, picture-in-picture and background play never restart playback. Video draws on a SurfaceView (Media3 Compose PlayerSurface). Decoder modes HW, HW+ (default) and SW, with a switcher in the player's Tools panel and a default in Settings. HLS, DASH and progressive links, with per-link HTTP headers.
- **Gestures.** A single tap shows or hides the controls at once; a quick second tap turns into a skip (10 s by default, more taps add more). Long press speeds up while held (2× by default). Horizontal swipe seeks with a time preview. Pinch zooms with panning, and pinching toggles fit and fill. Gestures never start inside the system gesture areas. Each gesture can be turned off in Settings.
- **Brightness and volume sliders.** MX Player-style glass bars on the left and right (sides can be swapped), shown always, on touch, or never. Speed-sensitive dragging: a smooth smoothstep curve on finger speed in dp per second, one step per slow movement with a vibration tick, the whole range in one fast swipe, and the value final at the moment of lift. Settings for acceleration, slow step, top speed, vibration, or a fixed linear slider. Brightness is the window's own brightness and goes below 0% with a dim layer. The hardware volume keys move the same slider without the system panel. Bars can be dragged or tapped directly.
- **Controls.** A translucent overlay built from the player layout model: back, title, play and pause, previous and next, skip back and forward, seek bar with buffered range, elapsed or remaining time (tap to switch), speed, aspect ratio, audio and subtitles, lock, rotate, picture-in-picture, queue and Tools. Media controls are not mirrored in Arabic unless "Mirror the media timeline" is on.
- **Tracks panel.** Audio on one side and subtitles on the other in landscape (stacked in portrait), plus video quality for adaptive streams. Tracks read like "Arabic · AAC 2.0" or "English · E-AC-3 5.1 Atmos". Long press on the subtitle button opens the same panel at the subtitle options.
- **Subtitles.** The app's own subtitle layer and cue model: SRT, VTT and ASS/SSA files from the file picker, from the calling app, or found next to the video with a matching name (including `movie.ar.srt`). Embedded text and picture subtitles (PGS, VobSub, DVB) come through Media3 and are drawn by the same layer. Delay in 0.1 s and 1 s steps, frame-rate correction (23.976 ↔ 25), drag to move, size, colour, outline, shadow, background box, bold and bottom margin, starting from the system caption size. Arabic: automatic encoding detection with ICU plus a Windows-1256 fallback, a manual encoding choice (also per file in the panel), and the "Fix punctuation" switch. Text runs in its natural direction with correct shaping.
- **Speed, aspect and zoom.** 0.25× to 4× with presets and a fine slider, pitch-corrected; default speed in Settings. Fit, fill, stretch, original, 16:9, 4:3 and 21:9.
- **Screen lock and orientation.** The lock blocks all touch except an unlock button that shows on touch, and can also block Back and the volume keys. Rotate button and an orientation lock in the player. Separate orientation settings for the menus and the player (phones only).
- **Picture-in-picture and background play.** Auto-enter on Android 12+ (on leave on older versions) with rewind, play/pause and forward actions; rules always, only while playing, or never. Background play keeps the sound going with notification and lock-screen controls when enabled.
- **Smart resume.** Positions are saved every 5 seconds, on pause and when switching videos, keyed by the media identity key (size plus a hash of the first and last 64 KB for local files), so moved or renamed files still resume. Resume automatically (with a "Start over" pill), ask each time, or never. Watched past 90% (adjustable) starts from the beginning.
- **Queue.** Videos in the same folder (or list) play as a queue; play next automatically (setting), previous and next, shuffle, repeat one or all.
- **Open with and external player.** Opens video and audio files, media links and HLS/DASH links from other apps, and links shared as text. Accepts the MX Player and VLC extras: title, start position (`position`, `from_start`), HTTP headers, subtitle files with names (`subs`, `subs.name`, `subs.enable`, `subtitles_location`), decoder mode (`decode_mode`), `video_list`, and `return_result`, which returns `position`, `duration` and `end_by` (and VLC's `extra_position` / `extra_duration`).
- **Settings.** New Player, Gestures and sliders, Subtitles and Local files sections, all searchable and with section reset, in English and Arabic.

## How it was tested

- Devices and Android versions: not tested on a device yet. The cloud build environment cannot run an emulator, and the owner asked for one APK at the end of all phases.
- CI run: unit tests (subtitle parser and encodings, slider acceleration curve, local sorting and sidecar matching, plus the Phase 1 tests), lint for both flavors and the standard-flavor licence check.
- Manual checks from the phase's "done when" list: pending a device.

## Known issues

- Software decoding (SW, and HW+ fallback) uses the device's own software decoders. The FFmpeg, AV1 and VP9 decoder extensions need native builds (a separate workflow) and come later; until then, AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS and TrueHD audio play only where the device has a decoder, and the tracks panel marks them "not supported" otherwise.
- Extra volume above 100% (boost) is Phase 3, so the volume slider stops at 100%.
- Matching subtitle files next to a video are found only where the app can read the folder. On Android 11 and newer that usually means picking the file once with "Load from file".
- Full ASS styling through libass is not in yet; ASS files use the simple renderer (text, line breaks, alignment, bold and italic).
- Thumbnail preview over the seek bar is time only for now.
- Subtitle delay applies to external files; delay for embedded tracks follows.
- Gestures can be turned on or off, but assigning other actions to them waits for the layout editor (Phase 11).
- Brightness and subtitle delay are remembered for the session, not yet per video (per-video settings are Phase 4).

## Decisions made where the prompt was silent

- "Automatic" player orientation follows the shape of the video (landscape for wide videos, portrait for tall ones); "Follow the system" uses the phone's rotation setting.
- Audio files are listed in the Local tab behind the Audio filter; the tab opens on Videos.
- Queues built from a long list hold up to 100 items around the one tapped, so starting playback stays quick.
- The player UI lives in the app module; the engine, local files and subtitle code are separate modules that the player reaches through PlayRequest.
- Following the owner's request, CI no longer builds APKs on every change: one build is made at the end, after all phases.

## Install

- Phone, tablet or TV: ORCA-Player-standard-v0.2.0.apk
- Extended build (sideload only): ORCA-Player-extended-v0.2.0.apk
- SHA256SUMS.txt lets you check the files were not changed.
