# ORCA Player v0.3.0: Phase 3, audio and video quality

## What's new

- **One audio chain.** Every source (local files, links, addons, extensions, downloads) runs through the same software chain inside the player, in this order: pre-amp, 10-band EQ (31 Hz to 16 kHz), bass boost, virtualizer, compressor (dynamic range, Late Night, normalisation), loudness, volume boost, limiter. It is a Media3 audio processor written in plain Kotlin, so it sounds the same on every device. It doesn't use the platform Equalizer or the Visualizer API, so it needs no microphone permission. Gain changes are ramped, so moving a slider never clicks.
- **Equalizer.** Opened from the player's Tools panel. Simple mode has the presets (Flat, Bass Boost, Treble Boost, Vocal, Movie, Late Night) plus the extra-volume slider. Advanced mode adds 10 vertical band sliders (drag, tap or D-pad), pre-amp, bass boost, virtualizer, loudness enhancer and compressor. The app remembers the last mode. You can save and name your own presets, save the current sound for one video or for its folder, and have it applied automatically when that video or folder plays. A live level meter in the accent colour reads from the chain.
- **Extra volume.** "Enable extra volume" in Settings → Audio lets the volume slider go past 100%, up to 150, 200 or 300%. The part above 100% uses the theme's warn colour (danger when the accent is yellow), with a mark at 100% and a "Boost" label. Above 100% the slider and the volume keys move in 5% steps. A built-in limiter (soft, medium or strong) stops clipping. A one-time warning appears above 150%. The Tools panel shows the boost with a reset to 100%.
- **Passthrough and spatial audio.** With Passthrough on, Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus (including Atmos), DTS and TrueHD go to the receiver as a bitstream, at 1× with effects off. If you then turn on an effect or change the speed, the app switches to decoded audio and tells you. The equalizer shows "Effects paused: passthrough" while a bitstream is playing. Spatial audio lets Android 13+ spatialize multichannel sound, with head tracking where the device supports it. The app's own virtualizer turns off while the system spatializer is active.
- **Normalisation and quiet-recording mode.** Normalisation evens out loudness. Quiet-recording mode (experimental, also a switch in the Tools panel) adds a noise gate, a hiss filter and a rumble filter for old cartoons and recordings.
- **HDR and Dolby Vision.** Video draws on a SurfaceView, so the screen switches to HDR by itself. If a Dolby Vision profile 5 video plays on a device without a Dolby Vision decoder, the player warns that its colours will be wrong. Profiles 7, 8 and 9 fall back to their base layer. A ranking function for the Phase 5 stream picker puts DV-only streams below HDR10 and SDR on such devices.
- **Tone mapping.** By default the system handles HDR. For screens where HDR looks washed out, Settings → Video quality offers the app's own GPU tone mapping (Reinhard or Filmic, as a 3D colour table through Media3 effects), with a brightness adjustment. It applies only to HDR video on screens without HDR.
- **Frame-rate matching.** "Always" switches the display mode to a refresh rate that is a whole multiple of the video's frame rate (24, 23.976, 25, 30, 50, 60, also on 120 Hz screens), at the same resolution, preferring exact rates. It respects the Android TV "match content frame rate" setting. "Only when seamless" uses Surface.setFrameRate through Media3. "Off" does nothing.
- **Decoder check.** New screen under Tools and Settings → Video quality. It shows every video decoder by codec, hardware or software, its profiles (HEVC Main 10 HDR10+, AV1 Main 10, Dolby Vision profiles and more), its maximum size and frame rate, and the screen's HDR formats and refresh rates.
- **Video info overlay.** A switch in the Tools panel and in Settings. It shows resolution, frame rate, codec, bitrate, network estimate, HDR format, decoder in use, dropped frames, buffer, the audio format and the audio output (normal, passthrough or spatial).
- **Settings.** New Audio and Video quality sections, searchable and with section reset (the audio reset also returns the equalizer to Flat and keeps saved presets), in English and Arabic.
- **Release safety.** The repository is public, so GitHub Releases now get only the standard APK, the standard AAB and SHA256SUMS.txt. The extended APK and the R8 mapping files go only into a workflow artifact kept for 3 days, and the latest-debug pre-release is standard only. A final tag (v1.0.0 and later) stops if any of the four signing secrets is missing.

## How it was tested

- Devices and Android versions: not tested on a device yet. The owner asked for one APK at the end of all phases.
- CI run: unit tests for the audio chain (flat chain is bit-exact, band and shelf responses, limiter ceiling, compressor, mono-safe virtualizer, presets), for Dolby Vision profile parsing, stream ranking and display-mode choice, plus the earlier tests. Lint runs for both flavors, plus the standard-flavor licence check.
- Manual checks from the phase's "done when" list: pending a device.

## Known issues

- Noise reduction in quiet-recording mode is a simple gate and filters, not RNNoise. RNNoise needs a native build and can replace it later, behind the same switch.
- Tone mapping through Media3 effects is applied when an HDR format starts. Changing the method while a video plays takes effect at the next video format change.
- Turning passthrough back on after it was paused for effects or speed happens at the next video, so a single video isn't interrupted twice.
- Bluetooth audio delay is Phase 9, as planned.

## Decisions made where the prompt was silent

- The chain keeps the decoder's sample format (16-bit or float) instead of forcing float output, which is the safest choice across audio sinks.
- Saved video and folder sounds are capped at 500 entries each; the oldest drop off first.
- When a video or folder sound is in use, equalizer changes update that saved sound, not the general one. "Use the general sound" removes the saved one.
- Frame-rate matching defaults to "Always", as the prompt asks for switching. It never changes resolution.

## Install

- SHA256SUMS.txt lets you check the files were not changed.
