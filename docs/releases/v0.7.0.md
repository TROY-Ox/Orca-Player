# ORCA Player v0.7.0: Phase 7, browsing and profiles

## What's new

- **Featured area.** Home opens with a large banner of featured titles: a carousel that moves on by itself and stops while you touch it, or one title, or nothing. Each slide has Play and My List. The catalog used is chosen in an add-on's catalog list with "Use for featured area", and is the first Home row until one is chosen.
- **Continue Watching.** A row of everything started and not finished, one entry per series, with a progress bar. Tap to continue (a downloaded episode plays offline), long press for the preview card, or swipe a card up to remove it.
- **My List.** Add movies and series from the title page, the preview card or the featured area. Home has a My List row, and the full list can be sorted by your order, date added, name or year, and reordered.
- **Cards and rows.** Cards can be small, medium or large, with three corner sizes, three spacings and a 2:3 or 3:4 poster shape. Rows snap to the first card, can show a "Top 10" style with large numbers, and the cards drift slightly as you scroll (off with Reduce motion). Home rows from every add-on can be ordered and hidden in Settings › Home rows.
- **Preview card.** A long press on any title card opens a card with the poster, the year and genres, and Play, My List, Mark watched, Download (movies), Details and Remove from row.
- **Profiles.** Add, edit, reorder and delete profiles, each with a name, a picture, its own accent colour and its own settings, history and My List. A profile can have a 4 to 8 digit PIN, stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash. Wrong tries make you wait longer each time, from 30 seconds up to 15 minutes. "Who's watching?" appears at start when there is more than one profile, and can be turned off.
- **Kids profiles.** They see only child-safe catalogs (adult add-ons are always hidden) and, if chosen, only some catalogs and local folders. The Addons and Tools tabs, the URL player, the clipboard offer, shared links and add-on installs are not available, and the player has no Tools panel and starts locked (a long press unlocks it). Leaving Kids mode, or opening Settings or Profiles, needs a parent's PIN or the device's fingerprint, face or screen lock. A daily watch limit stops playback with a "Time for a break" screen, and a grown-up can unlock it for the rest of the session. Screen pinning can keep a child in the app.
- **Skip intro, recap and credits.** Set where the intro, recap or credits start and end once, from the player's Tools panel, and every episode of the series gets a skip button.
- **Next episode.** Near the end, or when the marked credits begin, a Next Episode button appears with a countdown (5 to 20 seconds, or off). It plays the next item in the queue, or finds the next episode from the add-ons, keeping to the same release (binge group) when it can.
- **New-episode alerts.** Every 6, 12 or 24 hours (off by default), ORCA checks the series in My List and sends a notification when one has new episodes. Tapping it opens the series.
- **Settings.** New Home and browsing, and Profiles sections: the featured area, its timing, which rows show, card size, corners, spacing and shape, scrolling effect, animation speed, new-episode alerts, Who's watching at start and locking the player for Kids. Player gets the Next Episode countdown and skip buttons. Settings that apply to every profile say "All profiles". All of them are searchable and reset with their section, in English and Arabic.

## How it was tested

- Devices and Android versions: not tested on a device yet. The cloud build environment cannot run an emulator, and the owner asked for one APK at the end of all phases.
- CI run: unit tests (new: Kids rules, PIN hashing, checking and wait times, the daily limit, skip ranges from marks and chapter names, when the Next Episode button appears and its countdown, the next episode in season order, Continue Watching rules and My List keys; plus all earlier tests), lint for both flavors and the standard-flavor licence check. The database moves to version 2 with a migration.
- Manual checks from the phase's "done when" list: pending a device.

## Known issues

- Skip buttons come only from your own marks. Media3 does not read chapter names from video files yet, so chapters can't set them automatically; the matching code is in place for when it does.
- The featured area shows posters and backdrops only. Add-ons give trailers as YouTube links, which can't be played muted inside the banner, so there is no video preview.
- The card-to-details animation is a fade, not a shared element, for now.
- Animation speed applies to the featured area's motion; the rest of the app keeps the theme's springs.
- A child can still leave a pinned app with the system's own unpin gesture if the device doesn't ask for a PIN when unpinning. Turn on "Ask for PIN before unpinning" in Android settings.
- Taking off a kids PIN lock in the player is a long press, which a determined child can find.

## Decisions made where the prompt was silent

- One Continue Watching entry per series: the latest episode started.
- Skip marks are kept per series, not per episode, because intros are usually the same length across a season.
- The Next Episode button appears at the marked credits, or 15 seconds (or the countdown, if longer) before the end.
- Kids profiles with no catalogs chosen see every catalog that isn't marked for adults. Choosing catalogs narrows it.
- With no parent PIN and no device lock set up, leaving Kids mode is allowed and the dialog explains how to protect it.
- The main profile can't be deleted or made a Kids profile, so there is always a way back in.
- New-episode checks run only with a network connection and battery not low, and the first check of a series only records its episode count.

## Install

- Phone, tablet or TV: ORCA-Player-standard-v0.7.0.apk
- Extended build (sideload only): ORCA-Player-extended-v0.7.0.apk
- SHA256SUMS.txt lets you check the files were not changed.
