# ORCA Player v0.10.0: Phase 10, TV and large screens

## What's new

- **10-foot interface.** On Android TV and Google TV, text, cards and controls are drawn 20% larger than the same layout on a tablet. The app keeps the usual title-safe margins at the screen edges, uses the side navigation rail and the theme's lite motion tier. It is listed on the TV launcher with its banner, and the same build installs on phones.
- **Remote control.** Every screen moves with the D-pad and shows the accent focus ring. The player takes OK, the arrows, Back and the media keys, and its panels and Tools open from the remote.
- **Touch-only features hidden on TV.** Gesture settings, the floating window and clipboard detection don't appear on TV. List editors already have move up and move down buttons, which work with the remote.
- **Send to TV.** Addon URLs, CloudStream repositories, the URL player (link, referer, user agent, subtitle) and network share logins have a "Send from phone" button on TV. It shows a QR code for a one-time page served by the TV on the local network. Fill it in on the phone and the fields arrive on the TV. The page has a random address, answers only devices on the local network, and closes with the dialog.
- **Keyboard.** Space or K play and pause. The arrows seek and change volume, and J and L seek. F switches full screen, M mutes, C changes subtitles and A changes the audio track. [ and ] change speed, N and P go to the next and previous item, and Esc goes back.
- **Game controllers.** The D-pad or stick moves, A selects, B goes back, and the shoulder buttons seek.
- **Mouse.** Moving the pointer shows the player controls. The scroll wheel changes brightness over the left half and volume over the right half.
- **Foldables.** In tabletop posture (half folded), the video and subtitles use the top half and the controls stay on the bottom half. Playback continues through folding and unfolding.
- **Large screens.** On windows 840 dp and wider (the expanded size class), the media library shows the list and the selected movie or series side by side. Orientation locks apply only to phones.

## How it was tested

- Devices and Android versions: not tested on a device yet. The cloud build environment cannot run an emulator, and the owner asked for one APK at the end of all phases.
- CI run: unit tests (new: the phone form parser, HTML escaping, the QR encoder and the TV settings filter; plus all earlier tests), lint for both flavors and the standard-flavor licence check.
- Manual checks from the phase's "done when" list (a TV with a remote, a foldable, a keyboard, a controller and a mouse): pending devices.

## Known issues

- On a physical keyboard the arrows always seek and change volume in the player. Use Tab to move between controls.
- Long press on OK for the preview card uses Compose's built-in key long press. It needs checking on real remotes.
- Only the media library has a two-pane layout so far. Other screens center their content at a readable width.

## Decisions made where the prompt was silent

- The TV scale is applied once at the root, so every screen grows the same way. Fonts and colours are unchanged.
- The Send to TV page uses the theme's own colours and the default accent.
- Expanded width (840 dp) is the switch point for two panes, as in Material's window size classes.
