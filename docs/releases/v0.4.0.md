# ORCA Player v0.4.0: Phase 4, tools

## What's new

- **Tools tab.** Every tool works on any file without playing it first: pick a video (or a subtitle file for the editor) and the tool opens on it. A "Play a link" tool takes a video, HLS or DASH link with an optional Referer and User-Agent. A banner shows running jobs, and the Jobs list sits in the top bar next to Customize.
- **Tools panel in the player.** The three-dots button opens a glass panel that slides in from the end side (a setting can move it to the start side). It uses the theme's sheet spring, follows the finger, rubber-bands when pulled the wrong way, and closes from a flick or a drag past half its width, with the flick's speed handed to the spring. A scrim dims the video and the controls hide while it is open. In portrait it is a bottom sheet with the same behaviour.
- **Choose and order the tools.** Settings for the Tools tab list and the player panel list are separate: turn each tool on or off and move it up or down, with a reset.
- **Clip cutter.** A preview that loops the selected range, a timeline with two draggable handles and the playhead, Set start / Set end while it plays, exact typed times, and nudges by one frame or one second. Fast (copy from the keyframe before the start, and the screen shows where the clip will really start), Smart (default: Media3 trim optimization, which re-encodes only up to the next keyframe and falls back to a normal export by itself) and Precise (full re-encode). Saves as MP4 or as audio only (AAC/M4A) to Movies/ORCA Player/Clips, named with the title and time range, with Open and Share as soon as it is done.
- **Screenshot.** Copies the current frame from the video surface at the video's own resolution (not the screen's), draws the current subtitle in when that setting is on, and saves PNG or JPG to Pictures/ORCA Player/Screenshots with the title and time in the name. The player now draws video on its own SurfaceView so the frame can be read.
- **Subtitle editor and sync.** Edit the text and times of each line; add, delete, split and merge lines; shift everything; find and replace; change the text encoding (automatic, UTF-8, Windows-1256 and others); undo. Two-point sync: mark one line as A and one as B, type when each should start, and everything in between is stretched to fit. Saves as SRT, VTT or ASS to a file you choose, or to Documents/ORCA Player/Subtitles.
- **Audio extractor, converter and compressor.** Run with Media3 Transformer on the device's own encoders. The extractor copies AAC sound as it is and converts anything else to AAC/M4A. The converter changes codec (keep, H.264, or H.265 where the device can encode it), resolution, frame rate and keeps or removes the sound; with nothing to change in the picture it copies the video without re-encoding. The compressor targets 75, 50, 30 or 20% of the original size, optionally at a lower resolution, and shows the expected size and bitrate first.
- **Background jobs.** Cuts, extractions, conversions and compressions run in a foreground service with a progress notification and a Cancel action, one at a time in order. The Jobs list shows running, queued and finished jobs (kept across restarts), with Open, Share, Cancel and Remove.
- **Media info.** Container, length, size and overall bitrate; each track's codec, profile, resolution, frame rate, bit depth, colour and HDR, channels, sample rate, language and title; and tags. "Copy as text" puts it all on the clipboard.
- **A-B repeat.** Set A and B from the Tools panel; the loop is exact to the frame (a player message at B seeks back to A), both points show on the seek bar, and Clear removes it. Loops are saved with the video.
- **Sleep timer.** 15, 30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes, or the end of the current video, with a smooth fade over the last 10 seconds (a setting) and the time left shown in the panel.
- **Night mode.** An extra dim layer (20, 35 or 50%), a soft warm filter, and the compressor to even out loud scenes, all without changing the saved sound. Switch it on in the Tools panel, or let it turn on by itself between set hours.
- **Per-video settings.** Subtitle delay, subtitle and audio track language, subtitles off, speed, aspect ratio and zoom, brightness, decoder and the A-B loop are remembered for the video they were changed on. Precedence is video, then folder, then the profile's setting, then the app default. The panel shows whether the video has its own settings, copies them to the whole folder, or forgets them. The equalizer preset and boost already worked this way since Phase 3.
- **Saving rule.** Every tool that saves goes through the one saving rule: protected video and sources that don't allow saving are refused with a clear message, and links and add-ons show a one-time reminder first. Media info and the subtitle editor don't save the video, so they are always available.
- **Settings.** A new Tools section (panel side, remember per video, screenshot format and subtitles, default clip mode, sleep fade, night dimming, warm filter, compressor and schedule) and "Saving reminder accepted" under Privacy, all searchable and with section reset, in English and Arabic.

## How it was tested

- Devices and Android versions: not tested on a device yet. The cloud build environment cannot run an emulator, and the owner asked for one APK at the end of all phases.
- CI run: unit tests (new: clip names, time parsing and formatting, size estimates, sleep fade, keyframe snapping, subtitle writers for SRT/VTT/ASS, shift, two-point sync, split, merge, replace, and per-video precedence; plus all earlier tests), lint for both flavors and the standard-flavor licence check.
- Manual checks from the phase's "done when" list: pending a device.

## Known issues

- MP3 and GIF clips, MP3/FLAC/Opus/WAV audio and MKV/WebM output need FFmpeg. They are listed as unavailable and arrive with the FFmpeg add-on.
- Jobs on streamed videos don't send the link's HTTP headers yet, so links that need a Referer or cookie can be previewed but not cut.
- Converter track selection (picking one audio track out of several) and AV1 encoding are not offered yet.
- Media info doesn't list chapters yet.
- A-B markers on the seek bar can't be dragged yet; set them again with Set A and Set B.
- Audio delay isn't remembered per video, because the engine has no audio delay control yet.
- "Remember per video" is one switch for all the values rather than one switch per value.
- Screenshots of protected (DRM) video come out empty, as Android requires.
- Download current stream is listed and disabled until Phase 6.

## Decisions made where the prompt was silent

- Tools open in their own screen on top of the player, which pauses while you work and continues where it was when you come back.
- The subtitle editor works on loaded subtitle files. Embedded subtitles can't be edited, since they live inside the video.
- Saved files go to the shared gallery folders through MediaStore on Android 10 and newer, and through the public folders with a media scan on Android 8 and 9 (which asks for the storage permission once).
- The warm filter uses the theme's own warm signal colour at low strength, so no new colour was added.
- Night mode's compressor is applied on top of the current sound and switched off with night mode; it never changes the saved equalizer.

## Install

- Phone, tablet or TV: ORCA-Player-standard-v0.4.0.apk
- Extended build (sideload only): ORCA-Player-extended-v0.4.0.apk
- SHA256SUMS.txt lets you check the files were not changed.
